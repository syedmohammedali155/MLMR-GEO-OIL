🧪 Kya is tel mein koi chemical, milawat ya perfume shamil hai? ▼

Bilkul nahi! MLMR GEO Mustard Oil 100% khaalis sarson ke beejon se Cold-Pressed (Kachi Ghani) tariqay se nikala jata hai. Is mein koi chemical, bleaching agent, artificial color ya synthetic fragrance shamil nahi ki jaati.

⚙️ Yeh tel kis tarah tayyar kiya jata hai? (Production Process) ▼

Sab se pehle saaf aur behtareen sarson ke beejon ko chhana jata hai. Is ke baad kam taapman (low temperature) par lakri/steel ki ghani mein slow-press karke tel nikala jata hai. Aakhir mein cloth filtration ke zariye ise saaf karke 5L cans mein pack kiya jata hai.

🍳 Kya is ki tez boo (smell) salan ya khane ke zaiqay ko kharab karti hai? ▼

Nahi, yeh khass taur par Daily Cooking ke liye tayyar kiya gaya hai. Jab aap ise cooking ke liye pehle acche se garam (smoke) kar lete hain, tu is ki kachhi mehak khatam ho jati hai aur khane mein behtareen Desi zaiqa aata hai.
