Frequently Asked Questions

Clear and transparent answers to all your questions

📦 Can I order multiple 5 Litre canes at once? ▼

Yes, absolutely! You can select as many canes (1, 2, 3 or more) as you want inside the order modal. The total bill will be calculated automatically.

🧪 Does this oil contain any chemicals, adulteration, or artificial perfumes? ▼

Not at all! MLMR GEO Mustard Oil is extracted from 100% pure mustard seeds using the Cold-Pressed (Kachi Ghani) method. No chemicals, bleaching agents, artificial colors, or synthetic fragrances are added.

⚙️ How is this oil produced? (Production Process) ▼

First, clean and high-quality mustard seeds are sorted. Then, oil is extracted slowly at low temperatures using traditional wooden/steel presses. Finally, it is purified through traditional cloth filtration and packed into 5L canes.

🍳 Does the strong aroma ruin the taste of meals or curries? ▼

No, it is specially prepared for Daily Cooking. When you heat (smoke) it properly before cooking, the raw aroma fades away, leaving an exceptional traditional desi flavor in your food.

🚚 What are the delivery charges and delivery time for Rawalpindi and Islamabad? ▼

We deliver across all areas of Rawalpindi and Islamabad. Delivery charges are only Rs. 400 PKR, and parcels are typically delivered to your doorstep within 24 to 48 hours after order confirmation. (Delivery charges for other cities will be shared on WhatsApp).
